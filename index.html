    Speech engine

    Drop in a book.
    Hear it read aloud.

    Your file never leaves this browser tab. A voice model runs on your own device. Press play and follow along, one sentence at a time.

    Works with PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT and HTML. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) and DRM-protected books can’t be read. Includes English and Spanish voices; voice quality depends on the selected language and voice.

    The first time you press play, the voice model downloads (about 90 to 330 MB, depending on your device) and is saved for next time.
